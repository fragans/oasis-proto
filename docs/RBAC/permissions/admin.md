# Admin Role

The **Admin** role is designed for users who manage a specific organization. They have full control over the resources and team members within their active organization but cannot access data from other organizations.

## Permissions

### Campaigns (On Site Messages)
- **[FULL ACCESS]** Create, view, edit, clone, and delete campaigns within the active organization.
- **[WRITE]** Launch, pause, resume, and archive campaigns.
- **[WRITE]** Perform bulk actions on campaigns.

### Creatives
- **[FULL ACCESS]** Upload, view, and delete creative assets for the organization.
- **[WRITE]** Organize and manage the creative library.

### Audiences & Segments
- **[FULL ACCESS]** Create, view, edit, and delete audience segments.
- **[WRITE]** Define segment criteria and targeting rules.

### Team Management
- **[WRITE]** Invite new members to the organization.
- **[WRITE]** Change member roles (Admin or Viewer) within the organization.
- **[WRITE]** Remove members from the team.
- **[READ]** View organization-specific audit logs and activity.

## UI Capabilities
Admins have full visibility and control within their organizational context:
- All action buttons and menus (New Message, Edit, Clone, Delete) are visible.
- Full access to all steps in the campaign wizard.
- Access to team management settings for their organization.
- Restricted to the organization(s) they are members of; no global view or cross-org access.
